A Microsoft-aligned framework for building adaptable, scalable solutions

1

Process identification

Define relevant end-to-end business processes using Microsoft’s business process catalog as a common starting point

2

Business language & taxonomy

Establish a shared process language that reflects your industry, organization, and operating model

Current state assessment

4

Future state vision

5

Process alignment & governance
